FluentFlyout uses the library's QRC decoder, XML compatibility helper,
QRC/LRC parsers and Chinese conversion. Its QQ/NetEase HTTP adapters follow
the documented library protocols, with separate cancellation-aware transport.
The upstream library is unmodified. The FluentFlyout application remains
GPL-3.0-or-later, with its original copyright and attribution retained.

Transitive packages: CHTCHSConv 1.0.0, Newtonsoft.Json 13.0.3,
SharpZipLib 1.4.2. See their NuGet metadata for attribution and licenses.
